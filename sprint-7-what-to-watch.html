STEP 7

WHAT TO WATCH

— Measure Progress and Define Results
Track what matters most so you know if the plan is working and when to adjust.
KEY INDICATORS TO MONITOR
Performance Changes
Are expectations being met?
Team Impact
Is the team more engaged and productive?
Behavior Consistency
Are commitments being followed consistently?
Communication Quality
Is communication improving in clarity and tone?
Business Results
Are outcomes moving in the right direction?
MILESTONES & CHECKPOINTS
Short-Term Check
Review initial changes and early behaviors.
7–14 Days
Midpoint Review
Assess progress and adjust if needed.
30 Days
Final Review
Measure results and decide next steps.
60–90 Days
POSITIVE AND NEGATIVE SIGNALS
POSITIVE SIGNALS
Follow-through on commitments
Improved behavior and attitude
Stronger communication
Increased accountability
Measurable performance gains
NEGATIVE SIGNALS
Broken commitments
Defensiveness or blame
Declining performance
Withdrawing or avoiding
No change in key behaviors
WHEN TO ADJUST
Adjust the plan if:
  • Progress has stalled or reversed
  • New issues arise that impact results
  • The original approach is no longer effective
PROGRESS OBSERVATION LOG REQUIRED
Update this log at every scheduled checkpoint and whenever a significant change or event occurs.
Minimum required at Day 7, Day 14, Day 30, and any major event.
