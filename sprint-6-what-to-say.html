STEP 6

WHAT TO SAY

— Have the Conversation with Clarity, Firmness, and Respect
SUGGESTED OPENING
“You are highly valuable to this organization, and I want you here. But your interactions with the leadership team are now affecting performance and trust. We have discussed this before, and today I need us to establish exactly what must change, how we will measure it, and what will happen next.”
IF THEY...
GET DEFENSIVE
“I understand this is difficult to hear. My goal is to help you succeed here.”
AGREE
“Thank you. Let’s define the specific behaviors and outcomes we will measure.”
BLAME OTHERS
“We’ll focus on what you can control and how it impacts the team.”
WHAT NOT TO SAY
Don’t argue about the past.
Stay focused on the facts and the future.
Don’t label the person.
Avoid character judgments or fixed identities.
Don’t threaten impulsively.
Consequences must be clear, measured, and fair.
Don’t over-explain.
Keep it simple, concise, and direct.
Don’t make it personal.
Focus on behavior and impact, not personality.
CONVERSATION OBJECTIVES
  • State the concern clearly.
  • Define the behavior that must change.
  • Explain the impact on the team.
  • Set measurable expectations.
  • Confirm accountability and timing.
TONE TO MAINTAIN
  • Lead with composure, not emotion.
  • Be direct without being hostile.
  • Stay factual and specific.
  • Keep the focus on behavior and impact.
  • End with clarity about next steps.
