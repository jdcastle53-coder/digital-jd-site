Roadmap from now to resolution.

Selected Path:
Option B — Formal Behavioral Intervention.
1
NOW–24 HOURS
IMMEDIATE ACTION
  • Document 3 specific examples of behavior
  • Define the performance concern in measurable terms
  • Prepare for the accountability conversation
2
24–72 HOURS
 
  • Schedule and hold the accountability meeting
  • State clear expectations
  • Confirm the 30-day improvement review
3
DAY 4–30
 
  • Monitor interactions and behavior weekly
  • Give immediate feedback on setbacks
  • Reinforce positive changes quickly
  • Track progress indicators
4
DAY 30 REVIEW
 
  • Score progress against indicators
  • Decide: continue, refocus, or escalate
  • Communicate outcome and next expectations
5
DAY 31–90
 
  • If improving, reinforce and normalize standards
  • If partially improving, refocus coaching on weak areas
  • Continue monitoring and documentation
6
DAY 90–180+
 
  • Final evaluation of sustained change
  • Decide long-term path: retain, restructure, or separate
  • Document the final decision and next steps
EXECUTION PRIORITIES
1. CLARITY
Define exactly what must change.
2. CONSISTENCY
Respond to behavior immediately and evenly.
3. FOLLOW-THROUGH
Make checkpoint decisions on time.
WHAT THIS ROADMAP DELIVERS
Clear sequence from immediate action to long-term resolution
Built-in checkpoints at critical decision points
Flexibility based on results
Structure without losing humanity
